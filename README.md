## Hey, I'm Denzel

Hiyaa, I'm Denzel — or denz! I'm an Information Systems sophomore at Singapore Management University and an aspiring software engineer. I enjoy building projects, trying new technologies and learning as I go, especially in full-stack development and AI.

I'm also interested in cybersecurity, particularly red-teaming. I've hosted and participated in CTFs, and represented SMU's [WhiteHat Society](https://github.com/Whitehat-Society) at DEF CON 2026. Away from the keyboard, you'll often find me with a camera, shooting portraits, cosplay and events.
